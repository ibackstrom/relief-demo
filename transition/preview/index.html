Sand Transition

Preview

Scrolling down, the first image comes apart like sand: the next image opens through it in several places at once, and grains of the first image fall away from every edge. Scroll inside the frame, or use the buttons below.

Open full screen ↗
Variant
01

Opens in several places

On scroll, the new image breaks through at several points at the same moment. Each opening appears in a new spot every time.

02

Organic edges

The openings grow with an irregular, cellular edge rather than clean circles, until they meet and the new image fills the screen.

03

Falling sand

Along every edge the first image crumbles into fine grains in its own colours, which fall away under gravity.

04

Reversible

Scrolling back up closes the openings and returns to the first image. Works with mouse wheel, trackpad, touch swipe and keyboard.

Demo images are placeholders; any pair of images can be used.
